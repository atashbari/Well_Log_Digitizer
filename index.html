YSU Well Log Digitizer

Version 1.0, October 2026

Designed by Vahid Atashbari, Geo Energy Resources Initiative, Youngstown State University.

This tool converts scanned or photographed well log curves into numeric data. Load an image, straighten and crop it, mark the four corners of the track, enter the axis values, then click along the curve. Points are saved as a CSV file.

Your images stay on your computer. Nothing is uploaded; all processing happens in your browser.

Accuracy depends on image resolution and on how precisely the corners and curve are clicked. Check digitized values against the original log before using them in any analysis.

Developed with AI coding assistance (Claude, Anthropic).
